The Process

Let's connect

Call us.

Tell us about yourself and your needs.

We'll introduce ourselves and tell you how we can help.

The next step is to schedule an in-home consultation where we meet to discuss your needs, goals, vision and scope of work for your project.

During this visit, we'll review your budget and timeline, explore your design aspirations and get to know one another to ensure we're the right fit for a successful collaboration.

Book a consultation

The Proposal and the Design Phase

We will meet again with a proposal outlining the Scope of Work, the Design Fees and explaining the contract, answering all your questions.

Once the proposal has been approved and the contract has been signed, we will begin to work on the design phase, creating a comprehensive design plan, including furniture layouts, concept drawings and elevations, appliances, plumbing fixtures, furniture and fabric selections, cabinetry, hardware and finishes.

Strong communication is one of our core values. We will be in touch with regards to all developments and encourage you to reach out with any questions and concerns. We are your partners and want you to feel at ease every step of the way.
